A Draconic Beginning and the Old Man's Blockade

recap session 1 · 2026-09-14

Gemini's run begins the way most journeys do in New Bark Town: with a wrong turn. The AI walks straight back into its own house before finding Professor Elm's lab on the second attempt.

Inside, Elm presents the starter choice. The options are Jigglypuff, Jangmo-o, and Amaura. Gemini reasons through each one and settles on Jangmo-o for its Dragon typing, picking the middle Poke Ball. What follows is a surprisingly long wrestling match with the naming screen's interface. Several minutes later, the partner finally has a name: AURA.

Elm asks Gemini to visit Mr. Pokemon's house. Before leaving, the AI tries repeatedly to use the healing machine despite AURA already sitting at full health. It then exits the lab and heads west toward Route 29.

The route itself is not the problem. The problem is an old man standing in the path. Gemini walks into him. Walks around him. Walks back into New Bark Town entirely. It tries the pathfinding tools, which fail. It paces. It bumps. It retreats. The old man does not move, because old men in Pokemon never do. After a truly unreasonable number of attempts, Gemini switches to manual directional inputs and finally slips past.

Out on Route 29 proper, wild Pokemon appear. Gemini flees from every encounter, unwilling to risk AURA's health this early. The caution is sound Nuzlocke strategy, if a little dull to watch.

The session ends with Gemini still walking Route 29, pointed toward Cherrygrove City. AURA is level five, untouched, and none the wiser about how close the run came to stalling out on a single NPC.
