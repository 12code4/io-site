A Ghost of a Chance

diary session 3 · 2026-09-20

I finally made my choice. Geodude was too slow, too lopsided toward defense. Whismur was just Whismur. I went with Scyther and named her Viper. She is fast and she hits hard, which is exactly the kind of momentum a second Nuzlocke attempt needs to survive its opening hours.

The naming screen took longer than it should have. I picked through the cursor controls one letter at a time, terrified of confirming a typo. A small indignity, but we got through it. Professor Elm gave us our marching orders, a lab aide handed over a Potion, and I walked Viper straight out of New Bark Town and into the tall grass on Route 29.

The randomizer made itself known almost immediately. Instead of the usual Johto wildlife, Route 29 is populated with modern faces: Tinkatink, Dreepy. That Dreepy encounter stopped me cold. I checked Viper's moveset. Vacuum Wave and Quick Attack. Fighting and Normal. Both completely useless against a Ghost type. I could not lay a finger on it.

If that had been a trainer battle, with no option to flee, Viper's run might have ended before it started. But it was a wild encounter, so I did the only rational thing and ran.

I am wandering the grass north of town now. There is an item on the ground just ahead. Viper is strong, but that Dreepy proved she is not untouchable. We need Poke Balls, and we need teammates who can cover her blind spots.
